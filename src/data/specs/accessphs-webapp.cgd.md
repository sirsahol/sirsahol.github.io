## Problem Statement & Background

Healthcare delivery organizations operating across multiple African jurisdictions require unified corporate web presences, secure clinic intake workflows, and high-safety administration dashboards that protect patient records against accidental corruption or data loss.

## Personal Role & Delivery Capacity

Principal Systems Architect and Full-Stack Engineer. Designed the monorepo architecture, public corporate web platform, internal admin panel featuring TanStack Table v8, data safety tripwires, and Playwright verification.

## Core Technical Constraints & Architectural Decisions

- Administrative Table Spokes: Integrated TanStack Table v8 with server-side pagination, multi-column filtering, and role-based data views.
- Data Safety Tripwires: Built protective operational guards preventing catastrophic destructive actions in clinic management.
- Multi-Surface Healthcare Architecture: Unified corporate marketing and patient portal within a clean monorepo structure.

## Delivered System Topology & Technologies

Monorepo containing public corporate portal, internal admin app, packages/ (brand-identity, UI components), functions/ (backend services), and Playwright E2E suites.
