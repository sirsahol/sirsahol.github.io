## Problem Statement & Background

Luxury cultural e-commerce requires deep editorial storytelling and cultural journalism to contextualize artisan craft, designer lineage, and indigenous textile histories. Moremi Editorial provides an editorial companion publication platform designed for long-form reading, high-resolution visual curation, and seamless commerce integration.

## Personal Role & Delivery Capacity

Principal Systems Architect and Implementer. Designed the content management architecture, typography hierarchy, Turborepo integration, and automated code-quality pipelines (ESLint, Prettier).

## Core Technical Constraints & Architectural Decisions

- Performance & Content Delivery: Optimized editorial typography, Cold-Pressed visual styling, and static content generation.
- Monorepo Synergy: Designed to share UI tokens and domain models with the primary Moremi marketplace monorepo.
- Code Quality Gates: Automated Prettier and ESLint linting within Turborepo build pipeline.

## Delivered System Topology & Technologies

Turborepo monorepo housing editorial publishing applications, shared typography packages, and AST graph maps.
