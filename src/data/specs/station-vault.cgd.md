## Problem Statement & Background

Station-Vault (station-vault, alias svault) is a high-performance, zero-leakage secrets management engine and native Model Context Protocol (MCP) server written in Rust.

## Personal Role & Delivery Capacity

Saheed served as Author & Lead Architect, directing the architectural design, implementation, and deployment of `station-vault`.

## Core Technical Constraints & Architectural Decisions

Engineered for local-first reliability, high runtime throughput, strict zero-telemetry boundaries, and seamless integration across workstation nodes.

## Delivered System Topology & Technologies

Built with Rust, native process isolation, event-driven asynchronous queues, and automated verification suites.
